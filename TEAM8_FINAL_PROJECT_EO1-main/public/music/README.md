# Music Folder

This folder contains the background music tracks for your Sipa Rhythm Game.

## How to Add Your Music

Simply place your MP3 files in this folder with these exact names:

- `rock.mp3` - Rock Anthem (500 coins)
- `jazz.mp3` - Jazz Fusion (1000 coins)
- `classical.mp3` - Classical Rush (1500 coins)
- `electronic.mp3` - Electronic Drop (2000 coins)

## Important Notes

1. **File Format**: Use MP3 format for best compatibility
2. **File Names**: Must match exactly (lowercase, .mp3 extension)
3. **File Size**: Keep files under 10MB for faster loading
4. **Loop-Friendly**: Choose songs that loop well for continuous gameplay

## Default Track

The "Synthwave Beat" (free track) uses synthesized audio and doesn't require a file.

## Testing

After adding your MP3 files:
1. Refresh the game
2. Open the Shop
3. Go to "Music Tracks" tab
4. Buy and equip your tracks
5. Start playing to hear your music!

Enjoy your custom soundtrack! 🎵
